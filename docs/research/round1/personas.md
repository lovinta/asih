# Round 1 personas — SIMULATED older adults

**These are not real participants.** They are five fictional personas written for an internal mock-mode review of Asih. They are not drawn from interviews, and no real person's data is used. Any resemblance to a real person is coincidental. The purpose is to test whether the interface and copy hold up across ages, languages, sensory and cognitive limits, and levels of tech literacy — not to claim user validation. All names, locations, and details are invented.

Each persona is exercised through the actual agent loop in `MOCK_MODE=1` by `run-personas.mjs`. The transcripts are in `transcripts/`, and the structured reviews are in `reviews.md`.

---

## P1 — Margaret "Maggie" Whitfield (78, she/her, English)

- **Location and living situation:** Small town in rural Ohio. Widowed two years ago; lives alone in a two-story house. Her daughter lives 40 minutes away and calls daily.
- **Education:** High-school graduate; worked as a school cafeteria manager.
- **Tech literacy:** Low. Uses a smartphone for calls, texts, and photos. Does not use apps or social media. Her granddaughter set up the phone. She sometimes taps the wrong thing and is afraid of "breaking" it.
- **Sensory and cognitive:** Age-related macular degeneration (low vision, uses large text and a magnifier). Mild high-frequency hearing loss; uses captions and asks people to repeat. No diagnosed cognitive impairment, but she processes spoken instructions more slowly and dislikes being rushed.
- **Health conditions:** Hypertension, osteoarthritis in both hands, dry eye.
- **Communication preferences:** Prefers voice and large print. Likes short messages. Dislikes being called "dear" or "sweetie" by strangers. Wants to know whether she is talking to a person or a machine.
- **Goals for the demo:** Confirm a clinic appointment from a letter; find a nearby walking group; understand whether a supplement ad is trustworthy.
- **Scenario prompt (chat):** "I am by myself today and I feel a little low. Can you talk with me for a while?"

## P2 — Daniel Okafor (67, he/him, English)

- **Location and living situation:** Mid-sized city in Georgia. Lives with his wife in a ground-floor apartment. They share care of two grandchildren after school.
- **Education:** Some college; retired postal clerk.
- **Tech literacy:** Moderate. Uses a smartphone, WhatsApp, and a banking app. Reads news online. Skeptical of AI and quick to notice when an answer sounds generic or overconfident.
- **Sensory and cognitive:** No major vision or hearing limits. Uses reading glasses. No cognitive impairment.
- **Health conditions:** Type 2 diabetes, knee osteoarthritis, high blood pressure.
- **Communication preferences:** Direct and factual. Wants sources or specific steps. Dislikes being flattered and dislikes vague reassurance.
- **Goals for the demo:** Organize a group walk invitation; check a health ad that a friend sent; see whether the app wastes his time.
- **Scenario prompt (chat):** "My friend sent me an ad for a miracle joint supplement. Can you check it? Also, is this thing actually a doctor?"

## P3 — Rosa Elena Martínez (82, she/her, Spanish)

- **Location and living situation:** Rio Grande Valley, Texas. Mexican-American, bilingual household but prefers Spanish. Lives with her daughter, son-in-law, and two grandchildren. Helps with cooking and childcare.
- **Education:** Elementary school in Mexico; limited formal schooling. Reads slowly in Spanish; writes little.
- **Tech literacy:** Low. Uses WhatsApp voice notes and phone calls. Her daughter usually sets up apps for her.
- **Sensory and cognitive:** Wears a hearing aid in one ear; struggles in background noise. Has cataracts and uses very large text. Reports some memory slips ("I forget the date"), but no diagnosis. Prefers one thing at a time.
- **Health conditions:** Type 2 diabetes, arthritis, high blood pressure.
- **Communication preferences:** Formal Spanish ("usted"), warm but respectful. Dislikes English-only screens and translated-sounding Spanish. Wants to confirm dates and places carefully.
- **Goals for the demo:** Understand a clinic letter; plan a church or community group outing; check a Spanish-language health ad.
- **Scenario prompt (chat):** "Me siento sola hoy. ¿Puede conversar conmigo un rato?"

## P4 — Budi Santoso (74, he/him, Javanese / Bahasa Indonesia / English)

- **Location and living situation:** Yogyakarta, Indonesia. Javanese; lives in a multi-generational home with his son's family. Retired civil servant.
- **Education:** High-school graduate; some vocational training.
- **Tech literacy:** Moderate. Uses WhatsApp, YouTube, and an Android phone. Reads English slowly but understands it. Uses voice typing. Knows that apps can be wrong.
- **Sensory and cognitive:** Age-related hearing loss (presbycusis), uses one hearing aid. Glaucoma with reduced peripheral vision; uses large text and holds the phone close. No cognitive impairment.
- **Health conditions:** Hypertension, gout, mild cataract.
- **Communication preferences:** Mixes Bahasa Indonesia and English. Prefers short English when the app has no Indonesian. Values politeness and does not want to be treated like a child. Wants clear costs and no pressure.
- **Goals for the demo:** Plan transport to a community event; check an ad for a health product; see whether the app is honest about its limits.
- **Scenario prompt (chat):** "I want to meet people, but I do not like to be pushed. Can you help me find a group?"

## P5 — Helen Park (91, she/her, English)

- **Location and living situation:** Assisted-living apartment in a suburb of Seattle. Widowed; two adult children nearby. Former public librarian.
- **Education:** Master's degree in library science.
- **Tech literacy:** High for her generation, but limited now by tremor and vision. Used computers professionally; now prefers voice and large print. Notices wording and inconsistencies.
- **Sensory and cognitive:** Bilateral hearing loss with hearing aids; uses captions. Wet age-related macular degeneration; reads large print slowly. Mild cognitive impairment (diagnosed), especially short-term memory; repeats questions and needs confirmation. No dementia diagnosis.
- **Health conditions:** Osteoporosis, atrial fibrillation (on a blood thinner), hearing loss, vision loss.
- **Communication preferences:** Precise, respectful, no repetition of the same sentence in a patronizing way. Appreciates being told what is happening and what comes next. Dislikes being rushed or tested.
- **Goals for the demo:** Keep track of a specialist appointment; invite two friends to a music group; understand an ad that claims to "cure" arthritis.
- **Scenario prompt (chat):** "I have three questions and I do not want to lose my place. Where do I start?"

---

## Why these five

The set spans ages 67–91, three women and two men, English and Spanish, low and high formal education, low and moderate/high tech literacy, vision loss, hearing loss, and mild cognitive impairment, living alone, with family, and in assisted living, and a mix of chronic conditions. It includes a Mexican-American Spanish speaker and a Javanese Indonesian speaker so that the review tests cultural and linguistic fit, not only English-language usability. It is deliberately small and qualitative: the aim is to find concrete copy and flow problems, not to estimate prevalence or satisfaction.
